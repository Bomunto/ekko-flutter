## 1.1.0

* `Ekko.screen`, `Ekko.log` and `Ekko.recordRequest` reach the native SDKs: the
  screens, the app's logs and its network calls travel with the report.
* `EkkoNavigatorObserver` names every route for ekko — add it to
  `MaterialApp.navigatorObservers`.

## 1.0.0

* First release: `configure`, `present`, `identifyTester` and `handle` routed to the
  native ekko SDKs on iOS and Android.
